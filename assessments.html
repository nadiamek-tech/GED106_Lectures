Lab work · 30%

Excel Lab Assignment plus Weekly Lab Work. The syllabus lists three graded lab assignments, spread across the semester, together with in-class lab exercises.

Lab assignmentWeek 6
Data Cleaning & PreparationCLO-2CLO-4

Task: Identify and address inconsistencies, missing values, and outliers to ensure data quality.

  • Keep the raw data; clean in a new sheet called Clean.
  • Record every change in a sheet called Log.
  • Flag doubtful values; never invent missing answers.
Lab 6 practice →
Lab assignmentWeek 9
Analytical Functions & VisualizationCLO-3CLO-4

Task: use Excel formulas (AVERAGE, COUNTIF, IF, VLOOKUP) on your cleaned dataset, then present the results in clear charts.

  • Each formula should answer a question about your data, with a one-sentence interpretation.
  • Charts need a title, labelled axes with units, and an honest scale.
  • Remove any column that could identify a respondent before sharing.
